Big Buck Bunny (2008), a 3D animated short film by the Blender Foundation (project Peach), directed by Sacha Goedegebure, music by Jan Morgenstern. A giant, gentle rabbit, Big Buck, wakes up on a sunny morning and enjoys the meadow, the butterflies and the apples, until three mean rodents, the flying squirrel Frank and his sidekicks Rinky and Gamera, harass him, kill his favourite butterflies and pelt him with acorns. The rabbit gives up his gentleness, puts on a helmet and patiently builds ingenious traps to take revenge on the three, in a series of classic cartoon-style gags.
